# Super Store Sales & 15-Day Sales Forecast Dashboard

**Author:** Vishal Gupta  
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Project Type:** Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

## Project Overview

This project presents a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Super Store sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional distribution, shipping methods, and product categories.

The report contains two pages:
1. Super Store Sales Dashboard
2. Super Store Sales Forecast – 15 Days

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders
- Average Shipping Days
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by Payment Mode
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales by Category and Sub-category
- Monthly Sales and Profit trends

### 2. Sales Forecasting

- 15-day sales forecast visualization
- Historical sales trends
- State-wise sales comparison
- Forecast estimates and future-period visualization
